Hi I'm Sara!
Behind my technical background is a curious problem-solver driven by discipline, creativity, and a passion for modern technology. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and am currently pursuing my Master's, with my thesis focused on Deep Learning for Camera Traps on Embedded GPU Systems. I also focus on project execution, process design, and IT strategy.

I really enjoy bridging people, processes, and tech, especially through topics like Agile management and Artificial Intelligence. Communicating across cultures is a big part of who I am. I speak German and English fluently, Persian natively, and I'm currently picking up Japanese as a personal project.

I built this website as a portfolio project to challenge myself, learn new things, and have some fun. Ever since I was a kid, I've always loved creating things from scratch! If you check out the interactive parts of the site, you'll find the (AI-)Gorilla game. It's a sweet piece of nostalgia for me and one of my favorite parts of this project, reminding me of the classic QBasic Gorillas (DOS) that my siblings and I used to play together on our family PC.

When I'm away from my computer, you'll usually find me playing the cello, cycling, exploring new languages, or getting lost in a good book. By the way, I am a dedicated cat person and a big fan of Star Wars! My favorite character? Of course it is Darth Vader!
